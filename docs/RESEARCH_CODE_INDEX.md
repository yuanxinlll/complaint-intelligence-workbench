# Research source index

Source scripts are included as evidence and reproducibility references, not automatically executed workflows. They require the separately prepared local data/models/metadata named in their original paths. Do not run historical model-selection scripts against the reused validation to search for a better presentation score. New downloads, training and external actions require their own resource/data decisions.

- diagnostics/stage_c_20261028/prepare_v2.py: training-only conflict quarantine, preserving old outputs.
- diagnostics/stage_d_20261028/experiments.py: original limited word/character/SVC contrasts; English logs in this shared copy.
- diagnostics/stage_e_20261028/training_audit.py: started/completed/failed fit records, hashes, warnings and convergence.
- research/discrimination_20261028/stage_4_experiments/: bounded template screening and two fixed data-expansion fits.
- research/discrimination_20261028/finetune_run/: fixed FT01 training and read-only monitor. The shared future-run source excludes caches/environments from preservation and marks completion only after its final check; the historical experiment and original cache-warning logs are retained locally, not rewritten.
- research/discrimination_20261028/call_sensitivity/, caller_sensitivity/, phone_fallback/: recorded fixed diagnostic hypotheses; no default app patches.
- research/discrimination_20261028/three_direction_diagnostics/: synthetic channel/behavior, repetition and primary-issue wording probes.
- research/discrimination_20261028/reviewed_augmentation/: one fixed training contrast using reviewed synthetic pairs; actual review answers excluded.
- research/discrimination_20261028/presentation_evidence/top2_saved.py: rank already saved scores, no new inference.
- tools/keyword_probe/: user-supplied synthetic keyword probe and five input texts.
- tools/review_tools/: adapted portable review statistics and11 synthetic tests. Actual reviewer identities and answers are not included.

Aggregate results and figures are in docs/evidence/. That folder is explicitly allowlisted; real complaints, source IDs, per-record outputs, raw reviewer journals, vectors, checkpoints, environments and archives remain excluded. The complete owner-local workspace and Chinese teaching reports are not copied wholesale into GitHub.
